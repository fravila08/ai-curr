# Phase III – Intro to Full Stack w/Vibe Coding

## Overview

In this module we will cover two very fundamental concepts for this program. 

First, you'll dip your toes at a high level as to what Full-Stack Development is all about. Each lesson you'll discover a new portion of the "stack" and how it fits to the bigger picture of what todays web-development really looks like.

Secondly, you'll learn what AI is, how it understands you and how you can best leverage it to bring your ideas come true without having to necessarily know how to code.

The biggest benefit you'll receive from this module is an understanding of the full-stack application structure and exposure through practical application for building this infrastructure yourself.

---

## Modules

### [Intro to Full Stack Applications](./00-Intro-to-FS/README.md)

In this lesson you'll learn what is a full stack application what does it compose of and how these different parts communicate with one-another. This lesson will be vital to ensruing your understanding of the inner working of applications and build your own context for how you'll move about building your own projects.

### [Module 08 – Agents as Tools](./07-Classic-Chatbots/README.md)

In this module we will dive into the concepts of modern day AI. We will solve questions like What is AI? How does it work to understand me? Which AI should I use and when? and finally how could I best use AI to help me make my projects come true.
